Privacy Policy

Arusuvai — Your Recipe Companion · Last updated: September 2026

1. What Arusuvai stores

Arusuvai is local-first. Recipe data, favorites, recent recipes, culinary-dictionary entries and application settings are stored in the browser's local storage on the device/browser where you use the app.

2. No developer-operated database

The core application does not require a developer-operated database or application server. Normal recipe browsing, editing, searching, favorites and local JSON backup work locally in the browser.

3. Google Drive sync

Google Drive sync is optional. If you explicitly connect Google Drive, the selected Arusuvai backup data is sent to your own Google Drive using Google's APIs. The app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope for files created/managed by the application.

The developer does not receive a copy of the selected backup data through an Arusuvai server.

4. Google authentication

Google Identity Services is used to obtain authorization for the Google Drive connection. Google may process authentication and account information under Google's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current Arusuvai application does not include a developer-operated analytics database, advertising system or behavioral tracking service.

6. Backups and browser storage

Browser storage can be removed when site data is cleared, storage is reset, or a browser/device is changed. Use Arusuvai's JSON backup feature if your recipes or settings are important.

7. Revoking Google access

You can revoke Arusuvai's Google account access from your Google Account permissions page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's GitHub repository or the developer contact published with the deployment.
